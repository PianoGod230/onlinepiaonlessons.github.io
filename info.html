The Info

When you have lessons with us, you will be learning three main areas of study: Theory, Composing and Performing. Find out about each here.

Theory

Music theory is the backbone of a pianist's skill, as it is for all other instruments. You will learn this using worksheets, and generally it will not be covered much in lessons. You will learn:
  • Pitch, Melody and Harmony
  • Keys, Key Signatures and Modes
  • Chords and Cadences
  • Musical Terms (in Italian)
  • Musical Instruments and Their History
This is a very important aspect of your learning and you will struggle without it, so you must learn it no matter how boring it may be.

Composing

Composing is a less serious, more creative area of study, and this will be less structured as the other two. You can do this using manuscript paper and pencil or notation software, whatever works for you.

Performing

Performing is the biggest pillar of any pianist's building. This is further divided into 4 sections: Sight-Reading, Scales, Aural Tests and The Pieces.

Sight-Reading

This is exactly as it sounds, and helps you learn a new piece of music quickly. Additionally, if you get an impromptu request for a recital, you will be able to keep your cool.

Scales

This supports the theory learning described above. As well as developing your theoretical knowledge, this will help you hone in your dexterity and music technique.

Aural Tests

This name is rather misleading, as you will not actually be learning to sing. Much like listening tests, you will learn to recognise many of the features you learn about in your Theory homework.

The Pieces

The most important section. You get to choose the pieces, we will help you get them performance ready.

All of these together will prepare you for taking exams, performing in shows and competitions (though we will not force you to do any of this, we do encourage it), and turn you out as a well-rounded, highly skilled musician.
