The Options

There are 3 packages to choose from:
  • 20 minutes per week - £12.50 per lesson
  • 30 minutes per week - £15.00 per lesson
  • 60 minutes per week - £27.50 per lesson

Remember that you will have to commit to 1 month at a time. Because we teach 50 weeks of the year, you will pay for 5 lessons at a time, meaning you will spend £62.50, £75.00 or £137.50 at a time depending on your package choice. This makes the annual cost of lessons £625.00, £750.00 or £1,375.00 other companies may charge more than 200% of what we charge, and unlike some, you will be taught via video call by a real human.
